# Sales Data Analysis Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data using Python, SQL, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

The goal is to evaluate sales performance, product profitability, category performance, monthly trends, and the impact of discounts on profit.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and profit.
- Identify the best-performing products.
- Compare product category performance.
- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Evaluate the relationship between discount rates and profit per unit.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- SQL (SQLite)
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset

The project uses three datasets:

- `orders.csv` – order dates, customers, and cities.
- `order_items.csv` – product quantities, prices, costs, and discounts.
- `products.csv` – product names, categories, and prices.

## Key Findings

- Electronics generated the highest total revenue and profit.
- Accessories achieved the highest profit margin (42.57%).
- Headphones generated the highest revenue among individual products.
- December recorded the highest monthly revenue in 2025.
- Profit per unit decreased as discount rates increased in the analyzed data.
